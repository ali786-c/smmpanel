

STEP 1: Google Cloud Console
----------------------------
1. Go to the Google Cloud Console: https://console.cloud.google.com/
2. Log in with your Google Account.
3. Click on the "Select a project" dropdown at the top and click "NEW PROJECT".
4. Give your project a name (e.g., "EmazingSM Auth") and click "CREATE".

STEP 2: Configure OAuth Consent Screen
--------------------------------------
1. In the left sidebar, go to "APIs & Services" > "OAuth consent screen".
2. Select "External" and click "CREATE".
3. App Information:
   - App name: EmazingSM
   - User support email: (Your Email)
   - Developer contact info: (Your Email)
4. Click "SAVE AND CONTINUE" through the next steps (Scopes and Test Users) until you finish.

STEP 3: Create Credentials
--------------------------
1. In the left sidebar, click "Credentials".
2. Click "+ CREATE CREDENTIALS" at the top and select "OAuth client ID".
3. Application type: Select "Web application".
4. Name: Web Client 1 (Default is fine).
5. Authorized JavaScript origins:
   - Click "+ ADD URI" and enter: https://emazingsm.com
6. Authorized redirect URIs:
   - Click "+ ADD URI" and enter: https://emazingsm.com/api/auth/google/callback
   (IMPORTANT: Ensure the link is exactly as shown above).
7. Click "CREATE".

STEP 4: Copy Your Credentials
-----------------------------
A window will pop up showing your "Client ID" and "Client Secret". 

Please copy those details and send them to me in this format:

GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID=xxxxxxxxxxxx.apps.googleusercontent.com
GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET=GOCSPX-xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx
GOOGLE_REDIRECT_URI=https://emazingsm.com/api/auth/google/callback

Done! I will then apply these to  server.
